Baptized in Fear

<article class="essay-content section">

<div class="essay-body">

<div class="essay-stanza">
<p>
I fell asleep in the tub, I was met with paralysis
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
浴中阖眼，醒来时四肢皆已交付僵滞。
</p>
<p>
My foot hit the faucet, water started flowing in
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
脚踝不意碰开水阀，温水无声漫上来。
</p>
<p>
Couldn't scream for help, I just slowly felt the pressure hit
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
呼救噎在喉底，水压一寸寸咬进胸腔。
</p>
<p>
Moving one toe was the only form of motion left
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
全身意志，仅余一根脚趾的微颤。
</p>
<p>
Can't breathe for air, can't breathe
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
气若游丝，不进不出。
</p>
</div>

<div class="essay-stanza">
<p>
Trying to remember everything that my preacher said
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
拼命在脑中翻找牧师说过的每个字。
</p>
<p>
Tryna right my wrongs, my regrets filling up my head
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
欲正前非，悔恨却先一步灌满了颅腔。
</p>
<p>
All the times I dodged death, this can't be the way it ends, no
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
躲了那么多次死神——不该在这盆水里收场。
</p>
</div>

<div class="essay-stanza">
<p>
Figure in the corner I can't quite see (Quite see)
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
角落里立着一个人形，看不真切。
</p>
<p>
I just know the shadow's staring at me
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
只感觉到那团暗，正定定凝视。
</p>
<p>
It gets closer, it gets closer, it gets closer now
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
逼近了，逼近了，已到跟前。
</p>
<p>
Figure in the corner laughing at me (At me)
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
墙角那影子，在笑我。
</p>
<p>
Water fill my lungs, vision blurry
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
水灌进肺叶，视线涣散。
</p>
<p>
Heartbeat slower, heartbeat slower, heartbeat slower
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
心跳缓了，缓了，将歇未歇。
</p>
</div>

<div class="essay-stanza">
<p>
Voices'll tell me that I should carry on
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
有声音说：你该活下去。
</p>
<p>
Voices'll tell me that I should carry on
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
有声音说：你还要走下去。
</p>
<p>
Voices'll tell me that I should carry on
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
那些声音反复低语——活下去。
</p>
<p>
Voices'll tell me that I should carry on
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
它们说：别停，别停。
</p>
</div>

<div class="essay-stanza">
<p>
I've been baptized in fear, my dear
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
吾爱，这恐惧，便是我的洗礼。
</p>
<p>
I've been the chief of sin
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
万罪之首，是我。
</p>
<p>
Washing my soul within
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
趁此死生之间，向内浣魂。
</p>
<p>
I've been baptized in fear, my dear
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
亲爱的，我以惧为水，浸入重生。
</p>
<p>
Like Paul, I'm the chief of sin
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
如使徒保罗——我乃罪魁。
</p>
<p>
Washing my soul within, oh
</p>
<p class="meta-line">
一寸寸涤荡，自内而外。
</p>
</div>

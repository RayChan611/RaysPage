<p>你处在低谷期的时候</p>
<p class="meta-line">When you're down in the dumps</p>
<p>只需要在乎三件事情</p>
<p class="meta-line">Only need to care about three things</p>
<p>你自己，你的钱，你内心的平静</p>
<p class="meta-line">Yourself, your money, and your inner peace</p>
<p>生命周期性的暗潮只是暂时的</p>
<p class="meta-line">The cyclic undercurrent of life is just temporary</p>
<p>你正在死去</p>
<p class="meta-line">You are dying</p>
<p>但也将迎来重生</p>
<p class="meta-line">But also bring about rebirth</p>
